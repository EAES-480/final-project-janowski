# data

Place data file(s) in this folder.

Then, include codebooks (variables, and their descriptions) for your data file(s)
using the following format.

## name of data file

## data.csv

- `Do you believe global climate change is currently happening?`: Respondent’s belief about whether climate change is currently occurring.
- `Do you believe climate change is primarily caused by human activity?`: Respondent’s opinion on whether human activities are the main cause of climate change.
- `Do you believe climate change is currently negatively impacting the planet?`: Respondent’s belief about whether climate change is harming the planet.
- `How serious of a threat do you believe climate change is to life on Earth?`: Respondent’s assessment of the severity of climate change as a threat.
- `Natural resources should be conserved for future generations.`: Level of agreement with conserving natural resources for future generations.
- `Protecting the environment is more important than economic growth.`: Level of agreement with prioritizing environmental protection over economic growth.
- `Humans have the right to use Earth's resources however they choose.`: Level of agreement regarding unrestricted human use of natural resources.
- `Environmental regulations often go too far.`: Level of agreement with the idea that environmental regulations are excessive.
- `Governments should take stronger action to address environmental problems.`: Level of agreement with stronger government intervention on environmental issues.
- `Corporations are primarily responsible for environmental damage.`: Level of agreement that corporations are mainly responsible for environmental harm.
- `Individual lifestyle choices can significantly reduce environmental harm.`: Level of agreement that personal actions can meaningfully reduce environmental damage.
- `Would you support government regulations to reduce emissions?`: Respondent’s support for emissions-reduction regulations.
- `A carbon tax would increase gasoline prices but fund renewable energy. Would you support this policy?`: Respondent’s support for a carbon tax policy funding renewable energy.
- `If companies raise prices to comply with environmental regulations, who should bear the cost?`: Respondent’s opinion on who should absorb costs associated with environmental compliance.
- `A coal power plant provides many jobs but causes pollution. What should happen?`: Respondent’s preferred policy response balancing employment and pollution concerns.
- `How often do you try to reduce your personal environmental impact?`: Frequency of respondent’s environmentally conscious behaviors.
- `Would you be willing to pay more for environmentally sustainable products?`: Respondent’s willingness to pay higher prices for sustainable goods.
- `Which do you believe has the largest impact on climate change?`: Respondent’s opinion on the largest contributor to climate change.
- `Do you believe technological innovation will solve current environmental problems?`: Respondent’s confidence in technology solving environmental challenges.
- `Age range`: Respondent’s age category.
- `Education level`: Respondent’s highest level of education completed.
- `Which best describes the community where you currently live?`: Type of community where the respondent currently resides (e.g., urban, suburban, rural).
- `Which best describes the community where you grew up?`: Type of community where the respondent was raised.
